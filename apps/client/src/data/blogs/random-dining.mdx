After I finished my tutoring, I got invited by one of my students to have dinner with their family, and I was like "Sure! Why not?".

Normally I eat with my family or my friends. I am not sure how common this is for other people, but I rarely — or never — have an experience like this.

Imagine sitting alone, surrounded by people you are not familiar with.

### How should I behave?

How should I interact? Will it be weird if I do this?

I didn't know what to do lol. I don't know how to start a conversation with this many people, and I am not good at most of the topics they were talking about — medical, law, business.

So all I could do was go with the conversation flow and focus on my dining etiquette instead:

- ตักกับข้าวยังไงให้คนอื่นตักต่อแล้วดูน่ากิน
- ตักแกงไม่ให้หยด
- ควรตักกับให้คนอื่นไหม
- จัดคำข้าวให้พอดี
- ถ้าเคี้ยวข้าวคำใหญ่เกินควรปิดปากไหม

I used to practise only _western_ dining etiquette. So I had to use that to improvise the eastern one. (I don't know any eastern etiquette lol.)

Was I worried too much about everything? I don't know — but it should be reasonable to do so, since it was the first impression.

### An unexpected side effect

I just realised my memory was so good that I could recall a lot of details from a western etiquette course I took a long time ago:

- ช้อนแบบไหนใช้ตักอะไร
- มีดแบบไหนใช้ตัดอะไร
- แก้วน้ำวางยังไง
- ส่งขวดเกลือให้คนอื่นยังไง
- จับแก้วยังไง
- ปาดเนยยังไง
- เช็ดปากยังไง
- ตักซุปยังไง
- หั่นสเต็กยังไง
- นั่งยังไง
- วางแขนยังไง

Maybe I was so nervous that my mind became sharper. (I rarely use them, so I had forgotten most of it lol.)

They were so cute and nice together.
