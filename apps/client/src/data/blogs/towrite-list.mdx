### The list

1. **I should be thinking more about my travel companion.** (I was being selfish af.)

2. **I love the way the locals live and connect with each other.** (Eating บัวลอย ข้างถนน.)

3. **Opening a hostel might be a very nice idea.** (The owner chillingly lives on the first floor.)

4. **Remote working and travelling at the same time could fit me.** (I tried it and it was fun.)

5. **I found a couple kissing in the middle of the street** in front of the Royal Palace. Lol.

6. **Most people in Thammasat University used English**, wtf.

7. **Sports cars parked in front of sloppy buildings** in small alleys — Benz, BMW, all of it.

8. **I love walking alongside a canal or a river.** It felt so good.

9. **The Siriraj hospital museum** — an overwhelming amount of depressing and disturbing content, and also a look at how advanced our medical knowledge is. (Too long to write about now.)

10. **I got to talk to a soy milk seller.** (Learning how hard the selling is.)

11. **I am so bad at petting dogs.** They almost bit me. (I was so scared.)

12. **I want to try camping by myself.** (Many foreigners bring their tents to sleep in the hostel.)

13. **Walking alone at night in Bangkok is safer than I thought.**

14. **I accidentally stared at a lot of strangers** directly in the eyes, thinking they were my friends. (How to memorise people's faces, T_T.)

15. **Old school fashion and design are lovely af.** Imagine sitting in a place with old but clean and simple furniture — not luxurious ones.

16. **Seeing people interacting with others really calms my mind.**

17. **Horoscopes are fun though.** Idk. Maybe I just like deep talking with people.

18. **A group of foreigners asked me for directions.** I don't know why they chose me. Maybe pure luck, or I just look like a traveller.

19. **When I see something nice, I want to be fully inside it** — which means I don't touch my phone, and I fail to record any of it. That's why so much is lost. (It's also why I try not to use my phone when I'm with my friends.)

20. **Seeing men hugging each other made my heart skip**, wtf. That was so cute. Maybe I am gay, and I am fine with it.

21. **I prefer places with fewer people.** Chances to talk to the locals, plus the serenity.

22. **When I played football, I saw people listening to online classes while playing** (with Siriraj doctors-to-be).

23. ...
